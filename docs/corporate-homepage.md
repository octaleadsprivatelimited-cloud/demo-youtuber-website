# Corporate homepage refresh

The homepage below the hero uses a white and silver design with restrained navy actions and small RJ red accents. The shared header, hero markup, slideshow logic, global stylesheet, catalog routes, admin forms and CMS records are unchanged.

## Sections
- Catalog: white cards, real specifications, Popular/Latest/Upcoming tabs and working model/price links. Empty collections show an explicitly labelled research panel with an existing tractor photograph and horsepower filters, not fabricated listings.
- Research tools: three compact glossy white cards linking to the catalog, comparison and dealers.
- Brands: real CMS logos and actual model counts, including a deliberate single-brand layout.
- Comparison: white specification workspace linking to the existing comparison and EMI tools.
- Articles and videos: supplied records only; an empty article collection provides research navigation. Empty videos are hidden.
- YouTube: white invitation with a small red play mark and the configured channel link.
- Promotions: saved images displayed beside white copy, without dark overlays.
- Partners: supplied logos continue scrolling above the footer, with keyboard focus pause, manual pause and reduced-motion support.
- Footer: white corporate columns and a compact research action.

CSS is scoped to homepage component classes. Mobile layouts stack at 760px, while tractor cards retain touch scrolling. Hover effects respect reduced-motion preferences. No browser visual QA was performed because it was not requested.
